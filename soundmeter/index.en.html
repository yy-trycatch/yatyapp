Features

// core capabilities

realtime_db

44.1kHz sampling with Goertzel band analysis and A / C weighting. The gauge shows current, average, max and min dB plus a live trend line.

db_camera

Burns dB statistics, time, address, coordinates and weather into the video while recording. Front/back camera and flash supported.

history

Every measurement is saved automatically. The detail page charts the dB curve; tap any point to jump to that moment in the video.

export

Bundle an Excel sheet (summary + per-point data), the video and the audio, and send it through the system share sheet.

voice_trigger

Voice-activated recording: when the sound level reaches your threshold (70dB by default, adjustable 40-140dB), a 3-minute recording starts automatically.

i18n

Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese.
