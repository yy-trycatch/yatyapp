[soundmeter] v1.0.6 · Android 8.0+

Decibel Meter

Turn your phone into a sound level meter. Measure ambient noise in real time, record videos with a live dB overlay, replay every record and export it to Excel.

  • 44.1kHz sampling, A / C weighting
  • dB camera: live dB, time and place on video
  • Tap the history chart to jump to that moment in the video
  • Voice-activated recording, 6 UI languages

TimeStamp Camera

Stamp time, location, weather and altitude on photos and videos as you shoot. Built for check-ins, inspections, site logs and work reports.

  • General / Life / Work / Inspection / Attendance templates

Principles

// rules built into every app

local_first()

Photos, videos and measurements are stored only on your device by default. We run no backend that collects user data.

least_privilege()

Permissions are requested only when a feature needs them. Core features keep working if you deny optional ones such as location.

no_signup()

Use the apps without an account. Google sign-in is needed only to buy or restore Premium.

transparent()

Our privacy policies list permissions, third-party SDKs and data flows exactly as the code implements them.
